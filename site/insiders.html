Questions

Frequently asked

How quickly do I get answers to my questions?

Within 1–2 business days. Most questions get a same-day or next-day response. You'll receive an email notification when your answer is posted.

How does the 50% founding member discount work?

The first 10 people who sign up — whether monthly or annual — lock in 50% off the standard $50/month rate for as long as their subscription remains active. That means $25/month or $250/year, forever — compared to standard rates of $50/month or $500/year. If you cancel and rejoin later, you'd pay standard rates.

What kinds of questions can I ask?

Anything related to running a rural short-term rental — pricing decisions, guest situations, operational problems, review responses, tech stack questions, vendor issues, seasonal strategy. If it's not a fit for Insiders (e.g. it needs a full audit), I'll tell you and suggest a consulting session instead.

Is this right for me if I'm just starting out?

Yes — especially so. You'll have more questions than experienced operators, and getting solid answers early prevents expensive mistakes. The Q&A library will also build into a resource that covers the common launch pitfalls in detail.

What if I need more than quick Q&A support?

If a question needs a full strategy session or property audit, I'll say so and recommend a 1-on-1 consulting call. As a member, you get 10% off any session or package — just use your member code at checkout.

How does the member community work?

Once you join, you'll get access to a private community space where you can post questions, browse the Q&A library, and connect with other rural hosts. You can participate by email if you prefer — no need to log into a separate platform. Founding members get access immediately when the community launches.
